This is a worksheet to try with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It contains no collected responses or study results.

## What to check

1. Ask each tool separately: “We’re leaving the Musée d’Orsay around 6 pm on Sunday. Where could we walk for a vegetarian dinner without a reservation?” Keep the wording and language the same.

2. Log the date, search mode, places suggested and links shown. If there is no link, leave the source field blank rather than guessing.

3. Check walking distance, Sunday service and menus against the restaurants’ pages. Mark each detail confirmed, contradicted or not verifiable. This exercise cannot tell you which tool sends customers.
