This is an exercise, not a report of a test. Check whether a suggested address lets someone reach the right practice.

## What to check

1. Start a new conversation: “I’m staying near Bastille. How can I find a dental practice near the metro and check its Saturday hours?” Do not include personal medical information.

2. For each suggestion, note the practice name, hours claimed and link shown. Record it if no address is suggested.

3. Check details with the practice website or reception. A mention is neither medical advice nor a confirmed appointment.
