Finding a dentist and getting an appointment are separate steps. Test the connection without sharing medical information.

## What to check

1. Start on the practice website and follow the booking link as a new visitor. Check the practitioner, location and appointment type.

2. If the calendar does not accept new patients or lacks an appointment type, explain how to contact reception. Do not promise a slot on the basis of a summary.

3. Stop before submitting the form. An assistant’s answer is neither medical advice nor a booking confirmation.

## When booking leads to the wrong branch

Open the link from the map listing and then from the contact page. If one points to a group calendar, link to the correct branch where possible. Test the new link without creating a patient record.
